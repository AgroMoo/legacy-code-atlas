# Code of Conduct

We welcome respectful, evidence-led collaboration.

Expected behavior:
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique claims and evidence, not people.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Correct mistakes openly.
- Respect maintainers, contributors, and rights holders.

Harassment, intimidation, doxxing, discrimination, spam, and deliberate misrepresentation are not tolerated. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate this policy.
