# Internet Reclaimer — Open Initiative Vision (V1.0)

**We Discover What the World Has Forgotten.**  
**我们发现世界遗忘的价值。**

## 1. The idea

The digital world contains an immense body of human creation: software, models, datasets, products, research, inventions, creative works, virtual objects, and forms of value that have not yet been named.

Some are abandoned. Some are hidden in old archives. Some are misunderstood or poorly indexed. Some were created before the market or technology was ready. Some have value only when combined with a new tool, a new problem, or a new generation of people.

Internet Reclaimer exists to help people discover these overlooked resources, understand what they are, assess their real potential and constraints, and help them find responsible second lives.

## 2. Mission

Build an open, global, collaborative practice for digital value discovery and responsible reclamation — powered by human curiosity, domain expertise, shared methods, and AI assistance.

We contribute intelligence, research, tools, and care so that valuable digital creations are less likely to be lost simply because they have been forgotten.

## 3. What we mean by value

Value is not a fixed score attached to an object. It emerges from the relationship between an asset's capabilities, provenance, condition, rights, context, and a real need.

An old tool may become useful because a new problem appears. A dataset may become valuable because a new research method emerges. A discontinued product may contain a component worth reviving even if the original business failed.

The project therefore investigates multiple kinds of value:
- practical and technical value
- scientific and knowledge value
- historical and cultural value
- educational and community value
- economic potential, without assuming that every valuable asset should be commercialized

A discovery can be important even when it has no commercial value or cannot legally be reused.

## 4. What we are not

- Not a mirror that copies the entire internet.
- Not a claim over abandoned, dormant, or unclaimed property.
- Not a marketplace that assumes every discovery is for sale.
- Not a legal authority, investment adviser, or security certification body.
- Not an AI system that makes final ownership, rights, or safety decisions.
- Not a project that measures success by the largest number of records.

## 5. The shared method

Discover → Identify → Understand → Assess → Review rights and risks → Match to needs → Validate → Reuse responsibly.

Each step should leave evidence that another person can inspect and challenge.

## 6. Who can participate

Anyone can contribute: curious discoverers, historians, software developers, AI researchers, archivists, librarians, scientists, product builders, legal and licensing specialists, security reviewers, educators, and people with knowledge of a particular community or technology.

Participants do not need to join an organization or use one specific AI platform. The open Skill and repository standards are intended to make contributions portable across tools.

## 7. How we collaborate

- A public GitHub repository is the initial shared workspace.
- Issues are used for leads, questions, corrections, and proposals.
- Pull requests improve records, methods, templates, and Skills.
- The free Internet Reclaimer Skill helps individuals and AI assistants follow a common workflow.
- Specialist Skills and workstreams can be added as real needs emerge.
- The community can challenge, correct, and improve every assessment.

## 8. Stewardship and public benefit

The project is non-profit in intent at this stage. It is not currently a registered nonprofit organization, and no contributor is promised payment or financial return.

The project aims to create shared knowledge and reusable methods. Third-party assets remain subject to their own ownership, licenses, privacy obligations, and access restrictions. Discovering or documenting an asset does not transfer any rights.

## 9. A long-term aspiration

A global network in which a person can discover a forgotten resource, use an open AI Skill to investigate it, receive help from people with relevant expertise, and contribute a careful, reusable assessment to a growing public knowledge commons.

**A shared mission. A global community. A second life for forgotten value.**
