# Governance

## Decision principles

1. Evidence before claims.
2. Neutral cataloging: the Atlas does not adjudicate disputes over ownership or legal rights.
3. Human review for publication and high-impact decisions.
4. Corrections and takedowns are handled transparently and promptly.
5. No metric rewards raw volume at the expense of accuracy.

## Record states

- QUEUED — awaiting work
- RUNNING — active review
- REVIEW_REQUIRED — human or independent review needed
- ACCEPTED — passed the defined review gate
- REJECTED — reviewed and not accepted
- BLOCKED — cannot proceed safely or lawfully

## Evidence labels

FACT / INFERENCE / UNVERIFIED / CONFLICT / UNKNOWN. Every material claim should include a source, checked date, and concise rationale.

## Roles

Contributors propose and document. Specialist reviewers assess archaeology, value, and rights. An independent auditor checks evidence quality. Maintainers coordinate publication; they do not replace specialist review.

## Corrections and removal

Open an issue using the correction or removal template. Maintainers may temporarily unpublish a record while investigating. A removal request is not an invitation to retain or redistribute the removed material. Audit records should retain only the minimum operational facts necessary.

## Scope control

The MVP is the Legacy Code Reclaimer. New product lines, bulk ingestion, source-code mirroring, or automated rights conclusions require an explicit governance review.
