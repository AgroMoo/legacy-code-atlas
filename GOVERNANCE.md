# Governance

## 1. Purpose and character

Internet Reclaimer is an open, community-oriented initiative for discovering, understanding, assessing, and responsibly reusing overlooked digital value. It is non-profit in intent at this stage; it is not a registered nonprofit entity, does not promise returns, and does not claim ownership of discovered assets.

The repository is a shared working space. Its maintainers are stewards of the project infrastructure, not owners of third-party assets or final authorities on every domain question.

## 2. Core principles

1. Evidence before claims.
2. Discovery is not ownership; indexing is not permission to reproduce.
3. Preserve provenance and context.
4. Assess preservation basis and redistribution/reuse rights separately.
5. Make uncertainty visible: FACT / INFERENCE / UNVERIFIED / CONFLICT / UNKNOWN.
6. Use human review for consequential publication, rights, safety, financial, or access decisions.
7. Welcome disagreement, corrections, and independent replication.
8. Reward quality, usefulness, and responsible collaboration — not raw volume.
9. Keep the asset taxonomy open to unknown and emerging categories.

## 3. Decision model

- **Routine contributions:** maintainers may merge changes that follow published contribution and evidence standards.
- **Material asset assessments:** require source evidence and review appropriate to the domain; uncertain cases remain REVIEW_REQUIRED.
- **Rights, safety, privacy, security, or ownership disputes:** pause promotion or reuse and escalate to qualified human review.
- **Changes to mission, governance, licenses, data policy, or project scope:** must be proposed publicly, discussed, and recorded in a decision note.
- **High-impact actions:** no automated agent may independently acquire, transfer, sell, redistribute, access-control-bypass, or assert ownership over an asset.

The project initiator currently acts as the initial steward and may coordinate early decisions. This is not intended to create permanent unilateral ownership of community conclusions. Governance should evolve transparently as a sustained contributor community forms.

## 4. Review states

- QUEUED — awaiting work
- RUNNING — active review
- REVIEW_REQUIRED — human or independent review needed
- ACCEPTED — passed the documented project review gate
- REJECTED — reviewed and not accepted
- BLOCKED — cannot proceed safely, lawfully, or with adequate evidence

ACCEPTED means the record passed the stated project review; it is not a legal certification, safety guarantee, investment recommendation, or endorsement.

## 5. Evidence labels

- FACT — directly supported by cited evidence
- INFERENCE — reasoned interpretation, not directly established
- UNVERIFIED — plausible claim awaiting verification
- CONFLICT — credible sources disagree
- UNKNOWN — currently not known

Material claims should include a primary source where possible, exact location, checked date, and reviewer rationale.

## 6. Rights, privacy, and responsible handling

- Public availability does not imply permission to copy, redistribute, train on, sell, or commercially exploit an asset.
- Record creator/owner attribution and license evidence where available.
- Unknown or disputed rights remain UNKNOWN or CONFLICT; do not promote reuse.
- Do not publish credentials, private data, personal information, leaked material, or confidential business information.
- Do not bypass authentication, paywalls, technical controls, or repository restrictions.
- Blockchain observations do not confer control over wallets or assets. Never attempt unauthorized access, key recovery, transfers, or claims over dormant holdings.
- Respect takedown, correction, privacy, and security reports. Temporarily unpublish disputed material where appropriate and retain only minimal operational audit facts.

## 7. Roles

Discoverers submit leads. Researchers establish context. Domain experts assess value. Rights reviewers assess available rights evidence. Reusers validate practical outcomes. Independent reviewers challenge claims. Maintainers steward repository operations and community safety.

One person may contribute in multiple roles, but a reviewer should disclose conflicts and should not be the sole independent auditor of their own material claim.

## 8. Automation and AI

AI and Skills may assist with discovery, summarization, classification, and draft assessment. They must cite sources, label uncertainty, disclose limitations, and never represent an unverified inference as fact. Human review is required for consequential judgments and actions.

## 9. Corrections, disputes, and removal

Open an issue using the correction/removal route. Include the affected record and supporting explanation without exposing sensitive information. Maintainers may mark a record REVIEW_REQUIRED or BLOCKED and temporarily remove public access while a request is examined. A removal request is not permission to retain or redistribute the material elsewhere.

## 10. Scope discipline

The first vertical workstream remains historical software. Other asset categories can be explored through clearly labeled research and proposals. New categories do not automatically authorize bulk collection, copying, execution, acquisition, or commercial use.
