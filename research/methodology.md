# Discovery and Assessment Methodology

## Pipeline

1. Discover a candidate from a primary source.
2. Verify identity, provenance, and repository history.
3. Archaeology: determine what it did, its original context, dependencies, and historical significance.
4. Value: identify a current problem it could solve and estimate adaptation cost.
5. Rights: assess preservation basis and redistribution right separately.
6. Safety: inspect before execution; isolate tests.
7. Match: connect candidate capabilities to a concrete user problem.
8. Human validation: record whether a real user successfully reused it.

## Evidence discipline

For every material claim record:
- evidence label: FACT / INFERENCE / UNVERIFIED / CONFLICT / UNKNOWN
- source URL and exact location where possible
- checked date (UTC)
- reviewer and rationale

## Acceptance gate

A record should not be marked ACCEPTED until provenance, value, rights, and safety checks are complete. Acceptance means it passed this repository's documented review, not that it is legally certified or production-safe.
