# Rights and License Review

This project is an index, not a legal adjudicator.

## Two separate questions

- **Preserve basis:** Is there a defensible basis to retain descriptive metadata, provenance, and a link to the source?
- **Redistribution right:** Is there explicit permission or another verified legal basis to redistribute any source material?

These are not interchangeable. A public repository does not automatically grant permission to republish its contents.

## Default handling

- Unknown license: `UNKNOWN`; do not redistribute.
- Restricted or access-controlled source: do not bypass controls.
- Clear open-source license: record the exact license file/path and source revision; still check third-party assets and exceptions.
- No license file: do not infer permission from public availability.
- Takedown or rights dispute: mark `REVIEW_REQUIRED` or `BLOCKED` and pause promotion pending review.

This document is operational guidance, not legal advice.
