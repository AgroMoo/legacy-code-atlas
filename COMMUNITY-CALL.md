# Join Internet Reclaimer — Global Community Call

**We Discover What the World Has Forgotten.**  
**我们发现世界遗忘的价值。**

Internet Reclaimer is an open, community-oriented initiative to discover, understand, assess, and responsibly revive overlooked digital value.

We welcome people across disciplines—not only programmers. A valuable lead may be an old software tool, an AI model or dataset, an overlooked public dataset, a discontinued digital product, a research result, a digital archive, or something that does not fit today's categories.

## Why participate?

Digital work can be overlooked, poorly indexed, under-documented, disconnected from current needs, or difficult to evaluate. New technology and new needs can give old work a second life. But discovery alone does not prove quality, ownership, safety, or permission to reuse.

We want to build a shared, evidence-led process that helps people ask better questions and test responsible reuse.

## Who can contribute?

- **Discoverers** — point us to overlooked resources and explain where you found them.
- **Researchers** — investigate origin, history, current status, and context.
- **Domain experts** — assess technical, scientific, cultural, educational, or practical significance.
- **Rights reviewers** — help trace ownership, licenses, privacy obligations, and restrictions.
- **Reusers** — test a lawful, safe, and useful second-life application.
- **Independent reviewers** — challenge claims, verify sources, and document uncertainty.

You do not need to be a developer. A well-sourced lead, careful translation, historical context, license analysis, or independent review can be valuable.

## How to start

1. Read [VISION](VISION.md), [GOVERNANCE](GOVERNANCE.md), and [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md).
2. Browse the [first 10 discovery missions](docs/FIRST-10-DISCOVERY-MISSIONS.md).
3. Choose one mission—or submit a lead of your own.
4. Open an Issue with the source URL, what you observed, why it may matter, and what remains unknown.
5. Separate **FACT**, **INFERENCE**, **UNVERIFIED**, **CONFLICT**, and **UNKNOWN**. Invite another person to review important claims.

## Our safeguards

- Finding an asset does not make it ours.
- Publicly accessible does not mean free to copy, modify, redistribute, train on, or commercialize.
- Record permissions separately for access, copying, modification, redistribution, and commercial use.
- Do not access accounts, wallets, private systems, or protected data without explicit authorization.
- Do not publish secrets, personal data, exploit instructions, or sensitive access details.
- Pause reuse recommendations when rights, privacy, security, or ownership are unclear.
- AI tools may assist research; people remain responsible for consequential judgments and actions.

## What this is—and is not

Internet Reclaimer is non-profit in intent and community-oriented. It is not currently a registered nonprofit organization, does not promise financial returns, and does not claim ownership over discovered resources. Participation does not guarantee payment, employment, or other rewards.

We value careful negative findings as much as positive discoveries. “Not reusable”, “license unclear”, and “no evidence found” are legitimate outcomes.

## Join the first pilot

The first pilot is deliberately small: ten cross-domain research missions, transparent evidence records, and independent review. We will improve the method based on what contributors actually encounter—not on submission volume or vanity metrics.
