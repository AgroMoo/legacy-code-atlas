# Community Roadmap — First 90 Days

This is a proposal for a low-cost, evidence-led launch. It is not a promise of delivery dates or outcomes.

## Stage 1 — Make the invitation understandable (Weeks 1–2)

- Publish the mission, principles, contribution guide, and asset taxonomy.
- Publish the first draft of the free Internet Reclaimer Skill.
- Add simple issue templates for discovery leads, corrections, questions, and category proposals.
- Clearly label all sample records and planning placeholders.
- Ask a small number of people from different disciplines to review the mission and workflow.

**Exit evidence:** a newcomer can understand the project and submit a useful lead without needing to write code.

## Stage 2 — Test the shared method (Weeks 3–6)

- Select a small set of real leads from at least three different asset categories.
- Have different people or AI assistants investigate the same lead independently.
- Compare identity, provenance, value hypotheses, rights findings, and uncertainty.
- Improve the Skill and templates based on disagreements and mistakes.
- Publish only records that meet the evidence and rights standards.

**Exit evidence:** repeatable assessment reports with primary-source links, explicit uncertainty, and independent review.

## Stage 3 — Invite broader participation (Weeks 7–10)

- Publish beginner-friendly contribution tasks.
- Invite domain experts to review one category each.
- Encourage translations and examples.
- Recognize substantive contributions without speculative token rewards or volume leaderboards.
- Document moderation, corrections, and takedown handling.

**Exit evidence:** contributions from people beyond the initial maintainer circle, with a functioning review process.

## Stage 4 — Review and decide (Weeks 11–13)

- Review which categories produced credible discoveries.
- Measure review quality, correction rate, and validated reuse — not just repository stars or record count.
- Identify the most useful specialist Skills.
- Decide whether to continue, narrow, or revise the scope.

**Suggested early indicators**
- number of distinct contributors
- number of real leads with primary-source evidence
- percentage of records with rights status explicitly assessed
- number of independently reviewed assessments
- number of corrections resolved
- number of real, lawful reuse validations

## Guardrails

- Do not launch a token, speculative asset marketplace, or financial incentive scheme.
- Do not scrape or mirror large collections merely to inflate counts.
- Do not claim a global network exists before people actually participate.
- Do not describe the project as a registered nonprofit.
- Do not expand into sensitive asset classes without a specific safety and rights protocol.
