# Licensing and Contribution Rights

## Current status

The project is intended to be open and collaborative, but a repository-wide license decision has **not yet been adopted**. Do not assume that the absence of a license means all repository contents are free to reuse. Until an explicit license is added, default copyright rules may apply.

## Proposal for community review

A practical starting proposal is:

- **Code and executable tooling:** Apache License 2.0.
- **Original documentation, guides, and educational material:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
- **Original structured catalog metadata:** consider CC BY 4.0, subject to source rights, privacy, database rights, and attribution requirements.
- **Third-party materials:** retain their original license and terms; do not relicense them by inclusion or reference.

This is a proposal, not an adopted license grant.

## Contributions

Before adopting a license, contributors should submit only material they have the right to contribute. Do not submit copied third-party code, datasets, images, or documents without a clear lawful basis. A pull request does not by itself transfer ownership or grant the project rights beyond what the contributor is authorized to grant.

## Decision process

The project maintainers should invite public discussion, review compatibility with intended reuse, consider contributor attribution and patent provisions, and then record a dated decision before adding license files. If needed, seek qualified legal advice.

This document is project policy information, not legal advice.
